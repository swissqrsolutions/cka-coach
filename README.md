# CKA Coach

Practise every CKA task on your phone, then sit three timed mock exams.

Live: https://swissqrsolutions.github.io/cka-coach/ (open on the phone, tap **Install**).

- `content/cka-guide.md` is the single source. `node tools/build.mjs` turns it into `site/data.json`.
- Practice: tap the key words of the exam question, build the broken state in the free Killercoda CKA playground, solve, open hints one at a time, rate yourself.
- Mock exams: 17 tasks weighted like the real exam, 2-hour timer, flag/skip, self-check with the verify steps, weighted score (pass 66%).
